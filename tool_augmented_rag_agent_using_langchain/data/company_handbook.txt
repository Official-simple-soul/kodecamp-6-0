KodeCamp Travel Desk — Internal Handbook

Conference Travel Policy
All conference travel must be booked through the internal travel desk at least two weeks
in advance. Economy class is the default for round-trip flights under 8 hours of total
flight time. Hotel stays for conferences are capped at a mid-range rate and should not
exceed 4 nights unless approved by a team lead.

Expense Reporting
Employees must submit expense reports within 5 business days of returning from a trip.
Reports should include the total flight cost in USD, the total hotel cost in USD, and,
if the employee was reimbursed in Naira, the converted NGN amount using the official
conversion tool.

Support Contacts
For questions about travel bookings, contact the travel desk at travel@kodecamp.internal.
For questions about reimbursement, contact finance@kodecamp.internal.
